# UV-Control v0.4: design notes

**Status (2026-11-13): restarted from Rook v0.4.** Schematic done; the PCB is
Rook's board with the unused parts removed, and the new parts not placed yet.
The first draft is kept in [`reference/v0.4-draft`](reference/v0.4-draft).

## Pro Micro pins

Mapped 2026-11-17 to the board layout: U2's left column (pins 1-12) faces
J1/J2 and the analog side, the right column (13-24) faces the display and the
microSD. Nordic marks P0.02, P0.09, P0.10, P0.29, P0.31, P1.04, P1.06, P1.11,
P1.13 and P1.15 "standard drive, low frequency I/O only" (up to 10 kHz), so SPI
and I2C use the full-speed pins, all in the left column; their traces cross under
the module on the front copper (the module's pads are through-hole). Slow signals
fill the right column in the same top-to-bottom order as their destinations.

| Pin | nRF52840 | Net | Goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P0.06 | SD_CS | microSD CS (DAT3), R9 pull-up |
| 2 | P0.08 | MISO | microSD |
| 5 | P0.17 | LED_DRIVE | J3/JP1 pin 1; with 1-2 selected it drives LED_DRIVE_SEL: R3 to J2-3; R4 pull-down, C6 not fitted |
| 6 | P0.20 | EMIT_LED_DISABLE | J2-2 (HIGH turns the LED off) |
| 7 | P0.22 | SCK | display, microSD |
| 8 | P0.24 | MOSI | display, microSD |
| 9 | P1.00 | SCL | ADS1115, J4, R2 pull-up |
| 10 | P0.11 | SDA | ADS1115, J4, R1 pull-up |
| 11 | P1.04 | ADS_ALERT | ADS1115 ALERT/RDY, R11 pull-up |
| 12 | P1.06 | TFT_RST | display RST (firmware can reset the display on its own) |
| 13 | P0.09 (NFC1) | BL_OFF | Q1 gate: high turns the display backlight off |
| 14 | P0.10 (NFC2) | BUTTON_A | B1, R20 pull-up |
| 15 | P1.11 | TFT_CS | display CS |
| 16 | P1.13 | TFT_DC | display DC |
| 17 | P1.15 | BUTTON_B | B2, R22 pull-up |
| 18 | P0.02 (AIN0) | BUTTON_C | B3, R24 pull-up |
| 19 | P0.29 (AIN5) | DET_1V8 | J1-2, the detector's 1.8 V reference |
| 20 | P0.31 (AIN7) | | spare analog (e.g. to measure V_LED) |
| 21 | VCC | 3V3 | display, microSD, ADS1115, pull-ups |
| 22 | RST | RST | RESET1, R17 pull-up |
| 24, 25 | RAW, B+ | | not connected (no battery) |

The NFC pins (13, 14) carry only the backlight and a button: if the firmware
leaves P0.09/P0.10 in NFC mode, only those two stop working.

ADS1115 channels (address 0x48): A0 detect (J1-5), A1 rect (J1-4), A2
light-tight (J1-3), A3 LED current sense (J2-1). Each has an RC low-pass in
front: detect R5/C7 = 5.49 kΩ / 330 nF (about 88 Hz, as on control v0.1); the
others 4.7 kΩ / 100 nF (about 340 Hz).

LED pulse option (from control v0.1): U4, a CMOS 555 (TLC555) on 3V3, free-runs
at about 0.8 kHz, 50 % duty (R14 = R15 = 5.49 kΩ, C12 0.1 µF, D1/D2 1N4148W
split the charge and discharge paths; C13 on CONT, C14 decoupling). What drives
the emitter is chosen by J3 (0.1" header, for a shunt) or JP1 (open 3-pad solder
jumper), wired in parallel: 1-2 = MCU P0.17 (LED_DRIVE), 2-3 = the 555
(555_PULSE). With neither fitted, R4 holds LED_DRIVE_SEL low and
the LED stays off. Never select both ways at once. The 555 output and
P0.17 both swing to 3.3 V, about 26 mA in the LED. v0.1 used an NE555 on 5 V,
which needs at least 4.5 V and whose 5 V output would ask for about 40 mA.

J4 (not fitted): optional SSD1306 I2C OLED, as on Rook: pin 1 3V3, 2 GND, 3 SCL,
4 SDA. It shares the I2C bus and the R1/R2 pull-ups with the ADS1115; the OLED
is at 0x3C (or 0x3D), the ADS1115 at 0x48. The footprint's square pad is pin 4.

Test headers (0.1 in pin headers, each with its own GND pin for a probe or
logic-analyser ground):

| Header | Pins |
|---|---|
| J5 Power test | 1 GND, 2 3V3, 3 5V, 4 V_LED |
| J6 Detector test | 1 DET_1V8, 2 DET_LIGHT_TIGHT, 3 DET_RECT, 4 DET_DETECT, 5 GND (same order as J1 pins 2-6) |
| J7 Emitter test | 1 EMIT_LED_CURRENT, 2 EMIT_LED_DISABLE, 3 EMIT_LED_DRIVE, 4 GND (same order as J2 pins 1-3) |

The three sit in a column on the back, just outside J1 and J2 (J6 beside J1,
J7 beside J2, J5 below), with pin 1 at the bottom like the connectors. Because
the pin orders match, the traces from J1/J2 run straight across. The silkscreen
names each pin with the detector and emitter boards' own labels (1V8, LEAK, RECT,
DTCT; CUR, DIS, DRV). 555_PULSE has no test header: probe J3 pin 3.
