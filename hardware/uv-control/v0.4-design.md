# UV-Control v0.4: design notes

**Status (2026-11-23): U2 is becoming an Adafruit Feather.** The schematic has
the Feather (see [Feather pins](#feather-pins)). The PCB still has the Pro
Micro's footprint and routing until *Update PCB from Schematic* is run and U2 is
re-placed. The last version with the Pro Micro is commit `62d3ce3` ("last
supermini rev"). Open work is in [`TODO.md`](TODO.md).

v0.4 was restarted from Rook v0.4 on 2026-11-13; the first draft is kept in
[`reference/v0.4-draft`](reference/v0.4-draft).

## Where the parts come from

| From | Parts |
|---|---|
| Rook v0.4 (validated) | U5 ST7789 display (1.54 in footprint; TFT_BL1 since replaced by Q1/R12/R13), buttons B1-B3 and RESET1 with pull-ups R17/R20/R22/R24 (10k), J10 microSD with R9 (10k) on its chip-select, H1-H4 |
| First draft | U3 ADS1115 with R5-R8/C7-C10 input filters, R1/R2 I2C pull-ups, R11 ALERT pull-up, C11; J1 DETECTOR (now JST PH 7, pin 7 GND) and J2 EMITTER (JST PH 6); R4 pull-down on the LED drive (R3 and C6 since removed) |
| New | Test headers J5-J8 (0.1 in, replace the TP1-TP12 test points); C15 10 µF + C16 100 nF decoupling at the microSD supply (J10 pin 4); U6 TPS61041 boost for the UV LED (L1, D3, C1-C3, R3, R10, R16) |
| New (2026-11-23) | U2 Adafruit Feather (nRF52840 or ESP32-S3, M4 as fallback), replacing Rook's Pro Micro. The Feather has its own LiPo charger and battery jack, so the board has no battery connector. SW1 power switch on the Feather's EN pin, and J12 for a panel-mount switch in parallel |
| Control v0.1 | U4 555 pulse generator (now a TLC555 on 3V3) with J3/JP1 drive select; 5.49k/330n low-pass on detect |
| Rook v0.4 (again) | J4 header for the optional SSD1306 I2C OLED (footprint `ssd1306_a`), not fitted |
| Dropped from Rook | LoRa, GPS, the second OLED header, Grove, Qwiic, UART and 1-wire headers, battery input and switch, Rook's I2C pull-ups, and (2026-11-23) the Pro Micro |

UV LED supply: U6, a TPS61041 boost from 3V3, makes V_LED = 1.233 V x (1 +
R10/R16) = 1.233 V x (1 + 1.40M/160k), about 12.0 V, for the emitter's LED (J2-4).
R3 (100k) holds EN high, so it runs whenever 3V3 is up; J8 pin 4 can pull it low.
There is no 5 V rail.

J8 is also the place for Adafruit's 12 V boost breakout (product 5644: a TPS61040
with a 10 µH inductor, MBR120 diode and 875k/100k feedback, about 40 mA out at
3.3 V in), as an alternative to U6. Its header order, G, 12V, In, En, is J8's pin
order. Fit one boost or the other: both would drive V_LED, and their EN pins are
tied together. Symbol `uv254:adafruit_tps61040_boost`, footprint
`uv254:Adafruit_TPS61040_Boost_5644`; the 10.16 x 15.24 mm board lies over this
one on its header, 2.54 mm up.

## Feather pins

Mapped 2026-11-23 for an nRF52840 or ESP32-S3 Feather, with the M4 as fallback.
Firmware is moving to Arduino. On those three boards the Arduino pin numbers are
the silkscreen labels, so one pin table serves all of them. The symbol,
`uv254:feather` (from KiCad's `Adafruit_Feather_Generic`), names the pins by those
labels. The footprint, `uv254:Adafruit_Feather_on_socket`, is KiCad's
`Module:Adafruit_Feather` with a 3D model of the nRF52840 Feather plugged into
standard 8.5 mm female sockets: its underside is 11.04 mm from the board, and its
tallest parts about 18 mm. `uv254:Adafruit_Feather_on_socket_WithMountingHoles` adds
the Feather's four mounting holes (2.7 mm, for M2.5 x 11 mm standoffs) with copper
keep-outs around them.

| Feather pin | Net | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| RST | RST | RESET1, R17 pull-up |
| 3V | 3V3 | the board's 3.3 V supply (the Feather's regulator) |
| GND | GND | |
| A0 | BUTTON_A | B1, R20 pull-up |
| A1 | BUTTON_B | B2, R22 pull-up |
| A2 | ADS_ALERT | ADS1115 ALERT/RDY, R11 pull-up |
| A3 | | spare |
| A4 | BUTTON_C | B3, R24 pull-up |
| A5 | DET_1V8 | J1-2, the detector's 1.8 V reference |
| SCK | SCK | display, microSD |
| MOSI | MOSI | display, microSD |
| MISO | MISO | microSD |
| RX, TX | | spare |
| SDA | SDA | ADS1115, J4, R1 pull-up |
| SCL | SCL | ADS1115, J4, R2 pull-up |
| 5 | TFT_CS | display CS |
| 6 | BL_OFF | Q1 gate: high turns the display backlight off |
| 9 | SD_CS | microSD CS (DAT3), R9 pull-up |
| 10 | EMIT_LED_DISABLE | J2-2 (HIGH turns the LED off) |
| 11 | MICRO_PULSE | J3/JP1 pin 1; with 1-2 selected it drives EMIT_LED_DRIVE (J2-3) directly |
| 12 | TFT_DC | display DC |
| 13 | TFT_RST | display RST (firmware can reset the display on its own) |
| EN | PWR_EN | SW1 (slide switch on the board) and J12 (panel switch), in parallel: EN to GND = off |
| AREF, the pin after TX, USB, BAT | | not connected |

Why these pins:

- **DET_1V8 is on A5**, the only analog pin that is safe on all three boards. The
  ESP32-S3's A0-A4 are on ADC2, which Wi-Fi shares; its A5 (GPIO8) is on ADC1.
- **Pin 13 drives the Feather's red LED** (to GND through a resistor), so it carries
  an output. A pulled-up input there wouldn't read high reliably.
- **A4 is P0.02 on the nRF52840**, a "low frequency I/O only" pin: fine for a button.
- **AREF and the pin after TX** differ between Feathers (on the nRF52840 they are
  low-frequency pins, on the S3 not GPIO), so they are left unused.
- **Power switch.** SW1 (C&K OS102011MA1QN1, a right-angle slide switch, footprint
  `Button_Switch_THT:SW_Slide_SPDT_Angled_CK_OS102011MA1Q`) pulls EN to GND. J12, a
  2-pin 0.1 in header (1 PWR_EN, 2 GND), takes a panel-mount switch, such as a rocker in
  the enclosure, wired in parallel; either one in its off position turns the board
  off. The switches carry only the EN pull-up's microamps, so a switch with
  gold-plated contacts suits better than a mains rocker with silver contacts.
  Turning EN low turns off the Feather's 3.3 V regulator, and with it the whole board: MCU,
  display, ADS1115, detector, emitter and the 12 V boost. USB still charges the
  battery while it's off. The net is `PWR_EN`; don't confuse it with the boost's
  `EN` (J8 pin 4). The battery plugs into the Feather's own JST jack.
- **ESP32-S3:** firmware must drive `PIN_I2C_POWER` (GPIO7) high. The Feather
  powers its own I2C pull-ups and STEMMA QT port through it. Its battery gauge
  on I2C (0x36 or 0x0B) doesn't clash with the ADS1115 (0x48) or the OLED (0x3C).
- **Other Feathers:** the same map works on the M0 and ESP32-S2. On the RP2040
  (no ADC on A5) and the original ESP32 (A5 on ADC2) only the DET_1V8 reading
  is lost.

The GPIO behind each label, per board, is in CircuitPython's board definitions
(`ports/*/boards/<board>/pins.c` in github.com/adafruit/circuitpython).

ADS1115 channels (address 0x48): A0 LED current sense (J2-1), A1 light-tight
(J1-3), A2 rect (J1-4), A3 detect (J1-5). The order (swapped 2026-11-17) follows
J1 and J2's pin order around U3, so on the board every input runs straight to its
pin with no crossings. Each has an RC low-pass in
front: detect R5/C7 = 5.49 kΩ / 330 nF (about 88 Hz, as on control v0.1); the
others 4.7 kΩ / 100 nF (about 340 Hz).

Placement (back side): each filter capacitor sits at its U3 pin, with its resistor
on the J1/J2 side (C7 at A3, C8 at A2, C9 at A1, C10 at A0), and C11 sits right above
VDD (pin 8). SDA and SCL leave U3 to the right, between C11 and the pins, and ALERT
leaves from the bottom past R11. The 555 is about 10 mm below the ADS, with OUT
(pin 3) level with J3 pin 3, and its timing parts (D1, D2, R14, R15, C12) on the
side away from the ADS.

LED pulse option (from control v0.1): U4, a CMOS 555 (TLC555) on 3V3, free-runs
at about 0.8 kHz, 50 % duty (R14 = R15 = 5.49 kΩ, C12 0.1 µF, D1/D2 1N4148W
split the charge and discharge paths; C13 on CONT, C14 decoupling). What drives
the emitter is chosen by J3 (0.1" header, for a shunt) or JP1 (open 3-pad solder
jumper), wired in parallel: 1-2 = the MCU (Feather pin 11, MICRO_PULSE), 2-3 = the 555
(555_PULSE). With neither fitted, R4 (100k) holds EMIT_LED_DRIVE low and
the LED stays off. Never select both ways at once. The 555 output and
the MCU pin both swing to 3.3 V, about 26 mA in the LED. v0.1 used an NE555 on 5 V,
which needs at least 4.5 V and whose 5 V output would ask for about 40 mA.

J4 (not fitted): optional SSD1306 I2C OLED, as on Rook: pin 1 3V3, 2 GND, 3 SCL,
4 SDA. It shares the I2C bus and the R1/R2 pull-ups with the ADS1115; the OLED
is at 0x3C (or 0x3D), the ADS1115 at 0x48. The footprint's square pad is pin 4.

Test headers (0.1 in pin headers, each with its own GND pin for a probe or
logic-analyser ground):

| Header | Pins |
|---|---|
| J5 Emitter test | 1 EMIT_LED_CURRENT, 2 EMIT_LED_DISABLE, 3 EMIT_LED_DRIVE, 4 V_LED, 5 GND (same order as J2 pins 1-4) |
| J6 Detector test | 1 DET_1V8, 2 DET_LIGHT_TIGHT, 3 DET_RECT, 4 DET_DETECT, 5 GND (same order as J1 pins 2-6) |
| J7 | 1 GND, 2 3V3 |
| J8 Power test, or the Adafruit 5644 boost breakout | 1 GND, 2 V_LED, 3 3V3, 4 EN (the boost's enable) |

J5 and J6 sit just outside J2 and J1 (J6 beside J1, J5 beside J2), with pin 1
at the bottom like the connectors; J8 is at the bottom edge beside the boost. Because
the pin orders match, the traces from J1/J2 run straight across. The silkscreen
names each pin with the detector and emitter boards' own labels (1V8, LEAK, RECT,
DTCT; CUR, DIS, DRV). 555_PULSE has no test header: probe J3 pin 3.

## Notes

Open work is tracked in [`TODO.md`](TODO.md).

- **Symbols.** *Update Symbols from Library* clears the library-mismatch warnings
  (U5 `st7789`, U6 `TPS61041DBV`). The Feather symbol types its GPIO as
  bidirectional, so the Pro Micro symbol's five "not driven" ERC errors are gone.
- **Display.** U5 is the 2.0 in 240x320 module (GMT020-02-8P, 37.25 x 62 mm,
  footprint `uv254:st7789_200in_240x320`), on the same 8 holes as Rook's 1.54 in
  footprint, so a 1.54 in module with the same pin order still fits. Its courtyard
  is the whole module (it sits 2.5 mm up on its header).
- **Firmware** is moving to Arduino, using the [Feather pin map](#feather-pins).
  `firmware/uv-control/v0.4/code.py` (CircuitPython, first-draft pins) is out of
  date. ADS1115 channels: A0 LED current, A1 light-tight, A2 rect, A3 detect.
