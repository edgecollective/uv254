# UV-Control v0.4: design notes

**Status (2026-11-13): restarted from Rook v0.4.** Schematic done; the PCB is
Rook's board with the unused parts removed, and the new parts not placed yet.
The first draft is kept in [`reference/v0.4-draft`](reference/v0.4-draft).

## Where the parts come from

| From | Parts |
|---|---|
| Rook v0.4 (validated) | U2 Pro Micro nRF52840, U5 ST7789 display (1.54 in footprint) and TFT_BL1 backlight switch, buttons B1-B3 and RESET1 with pull-ups R14/R17/R19-R24, J10 microSD with R9/R10 on its chip-select, H1-H4 |
| First draft | U3 ADS1115 with R5-R8/C7-C10 input filters, R1/R2 I2C pull-ups, R11 ALERT pull-up, C11; J1 DETECTOR and J2 EMITTER (JST PH 6); R3/R4/C6 on LED_DRIVE |
| New | TP1-TP11 test points (Keystone 5010-5014 footprint) |
| Dropped from Rook | LoRa, GPS, OLED headers, Grove, Qwiic, UART and 1-wire headers, battery input and switch, Rook's I2C pull-ups |

Not yet in the schematic: the boost for the UV LED (a different one from the
draft's LMR62014). **5V and V_LED** have no source until it is added.

## Pro Micro pins

| Pin | nRF52840 | Net | Goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P0.06 | P0_6 | display DC, button B2 (shared, as on Rook) |
| 2 | P0.08 | P0_8 | display CS, button B3 (shared, as on Rook) |
| 5, 6 | P0.17, P0.20 | | spare |
| 7 | P0.22 | LED_ENABLE | J2-2 (HIGH turns the LED off) |
| 8 | P0.24 | LED_DRIVE | R3 to J2-3; R4 pull-down, C6 not fitted |
| 9 | P1.00 | BUTTON_A | B1 |
| 10 | P0.11 | SCL | ADS1115, R2 pull-up |
| 11 | P1.04 | SDA | ADS1115, R1 pull-up |
| 12 | P1.06 | P1_06 | microSD CS (DAT3), R9/R10 pull-up |
| 13 | P0.09 | ADS_ALERT | ADS1115 ALERT/RDY, R11 pull-up |
| 14, 16 | P0.10, P1.13 | | spare |
| 15 | P1.11 | SCK | display, microSD |
| 17 | P1.15 | MOSI | display, microSD |
| 18 | P0.02 | MISO | microSD |
| 19 | P0.29 (AIN5) | DET_2V5 | J1-2, the detector's 2.5 V reference |
| 20 | P0.31 (AIN7) | | spare analog (e.g. to measure V_LED) |
| 21 | VCC | 3V3 | display, microSD, ADS1115, pull-ups |
| 22 | RST | MESH_RST | RESET1, display RST |
| 24, 25 | RAW, B+ | | not connected (no battery) |

ADS1115 channels (address 0x48): A0 detect (J1-5), A1 rect (J1-4), A2
light-tight (J1-3), A3 LED current sense (J2-1), each through 4.7 kΩ + 100 nF.

Test points: TP1 GND, TP2 3V3, TP3 5V, TP4 V_LED, TP5 DET_DETECT, TP6 DET_RECT,
TP7 DET_LIGHT_TIGHT, TP8 DET_2V5, TP9 EMIT_LED_CURRENT, TP10 EMIT_LED_DRIVE,
TP11 LED_ENABLE.

  TP1-TP11, and renames the Pro Micro pads whose nets changed (pins 7, 8, 13, 19)

- **Symbols.** *Update symbols from library* clears the three library-mismatch
  warnings. ERC's two remaining errors (SD CMD/CLK "not driven") come from the
  Pro Micro symbol typing its pins as inputs, as on Rook.
- **Display size.** The footprint is Rook's 1.54 in. `uv254:st7789_tft_2in_320x240`
  is the 2.0 in one (same pin order, different outline).
- **Firmware.** `firmware/uv-control/v0.4/code.py` still uses the first draft's
  pins. The display moves to SCK P1.11, MOSI P1.15, CS P0.08, DC P0.06 with RST on
  the MCU reset, I2C to P0.11/P1.04, and DC/CS are shared with buttons B2/B3.
